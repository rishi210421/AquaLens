# Hackathon Demo Script: 3-Minute Walkthrough

**Target:** OneAquaHealth IEEE Global Hackathon 2026 (Track 2: Data-to-Insight)

## 0:00 - 0:30: Context & Challenge
- Open landing page (`/`). Point to the Track 2 tagline: *"From stream data to actionable One Health intelligence."*
- Highlight the problem: Raw environmental data does not tell citizens or city planners what changed or why a stream is deteriorating.

## 0:30 - 1:00: Live Dashboard & Hotspots
- Navigate to **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`).
- Point out the 6 summary cards calculated dynamically across the 5 pilot cities (Lisbon, Lyon, Bristol, Freiburg, Valencia).
- Identify the **Potential Hotspots** and show the trajectory time-series curve.

## 1:00 - 1:45: Interactive Map & Attention Reach
- Navigate to **Map** (`/map`).
- Toggle the **Hotspots** layer filter.
- Click on an Attention Reach, e.g. **Sacavém Estuary Outfall Station** in Lisbon or **St Jude's Culvert** in Bristol.
- Show the interactive popup with the calculated composite score (52/100) and click **Inspect Site Telemetry**.

## 1:45 - 2:30: Deep Dive ("What Changed?" & "Why Flagged?")
- On the Site Detail page, highlight:
  - **Composite Score:** 52.4 / 100 with High Confidence.
  - **"What Changed?":** The system pinpoints that the primary negative driver was a 16.2% decline in physicochemical water quality paired with a 24% surge in pollution reports.
  - **"Why is this site flagged?":** Shows the transparent root-cause breakdown and immediate recommended surveillance action.
  - **One Health Context:** Explains how dissolved oxygen depletion and runoff interface with urban vector ecology and greenway safety.
  - Click **Generate Natural Language Summary** to demonstrate the AI synthesis.

## 2:30 - 3:00: Natural Language Exploration & Reporting
- Navigate to **Ask AquaInsight** (`/ask`).
- Click *"Which sites declined the most this month?"* to show grounded question answering.
- Navigate to **Reports** (`/reports`) and demonstrate instant CSV data export and printable executive report generation.
